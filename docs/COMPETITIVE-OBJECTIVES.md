# GoreeCloud Dialer Competitive Objectives

GoreeCloud Dialer should meet the quality expectations of a modern Android dialer while preserving GoreeCloud privacy, authority, accessibility, and local-first principles.

## Objectives

- Make ordinary dialing, incoming-call handling, ongoing-call controls, DTMF, mute, hold/resume, and supported endpoint routing fast and understandable.
- Provide truthful multi-SIM and phone-account selection without exposing unnecessary carrier, subscriber, or device identity.
- Keep emergency behavior platform-safe and never manufacture application authority over emergency routing.
- Present only verified capability state; unsupported, unavailable, permission-blocked, role-blocked, submitted, failed, and succeeded states must remain distinct.
- Support accessible touch targets, large text, keyboard/focus behavior, RTL/localization, and representative-device form factors.
- Maintain useful core calling when GoreeCloud cloud services, Identity, Mesh, or other online systems are unavailable.
- Treat advanced capabilities such as screening, spam protection, voicemail, recording, transcription, translation, AI assistance, continuity, and cross-device calling as separately governed additions rather than reasons to weaken core-call reliability.
- Require privacy-safe physical-device and carrier evidence before claiming carrier/PSTN behavior is accepted.

Competitive quality must come from original GoreeCloud implementation and verified platform integration, not copied third-party source, branding, or UI.
